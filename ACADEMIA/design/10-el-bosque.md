# 10 — El Bosque: the Brote learning system

> The design of the Academia section. This is the *what and why*. `13-data-model.md` is the *how*.

---

## 1. The problem with the placeholder

The current `/aprender` is 10 hand-written lessons in three "levels", each a deck of `info | quiz | truefalse` cards. It has four fatal properties:

1. **It ends.** Ten lessons is a weekend. There is no mechanism for growth.
2. **It is the same for everyone.** No personalisation, no adaptation, no memory of what you got wrong.
3. **It teaches once.** Nothing brings a concept back. Everything is forgotten within a fortnight.
4. **It is disconnected.** Finishing a lesson about greywater does not lead you to the greywater action sitting in the catalogue two taps away.

Everything below exists to fix exactly those four things.

---

## 2. The metaphor: one tree, growing rings

Brote's whole product is a living thing that grows. The Academia is not a road with stops on it — it is **a tree seen from the side, growing upward and outward, adding a ring every season**.

```
                    ANILLO 3  ·  sistemas y decisiones
              ┌──────────────────────────────────┐
              │   ANILLO 2  ·  causas y datos    │
              │   ┌────────────────────────┐     │
   hojas ●────┤   │  ANILLO 1 · lo básico  │     ├────● hojas
              │   │  ┌──────────────┐      │     │
   gajo ══════╪═══╪══╡    TRONCO    ╞══════╪═════╪══════ gajo
              │   │  └──────────────┘      │     │
              └───┴────────────────────────┴─────┘
                         rama (dominio)
```

| Term | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| **Tronco** | The shared foundation everyone walks first. Systems thinking, what an ecosystem is, how to read an environmental number, why individual action matters and where it stops mattering. | 1 · ~5 gajos |
| **Rama** | One of the 13 domains already in `lib/domains.ts` (`residuos`, `agua`, `animales`, …). Colour = the domain's identity colour. Never invented. | 13 |
| **Gajo** | A themed cluster inside a rama — "Fauna de tu barrio", "La ruta de una botella", "Leer una etiqueta". A gajo is the unit a user says they are "doing". | ~6–10 per rama per anillo, **procedurally extensible** |
| **Hoja** | One lesson node. A session of 7–12 steps built at request time. A hoja is never a fixed deck. | ~4–8 per gajo |
| **Concepto** | The atomic teachable unit (a knowledge component). "El agua virtual de un alimento". Mastery is tracked **per concepto**, not per hoja. | ~450 at launch, unbounded |
| **Anillo** | A growth ring. Completing the reachable tree at ring *n* opens ring *n+1*, which revisits the same ramas at greater depth with new gajos. | ∞ |

**Why rings and not a longer line.** A linear path has a visible end and forces one order. A ring model means: (a) the tree is always finite *right now* so it is never overwhelming, (b) it is infinite *over time* because a new ring is generated rather than authored, (c) revisiting a rama at ring 2 is pedagogically correct — it is a spiral curriculum, which is what UNESCO's and NAAEE's scope-and-sequence guidance actually describes. It is also botanically true, which makes it Brote's and nobody else's.

**Depth per ring** (the spiral):

| Anillo | Cognitive level | Example in `animales` |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recognise and name | Which of these lives in the Delta? What is a native species? |
| 2 | Explain and relate | Why does habitat fragmentation matter more than total area lost? |
| 3 | Compare and decide | Two conservation budgets — which buys more biodiversity, and what does it cost? |
| 4+ | Systems and trade-offs | Generated by the pipeline against the anillo-4 rubric. |

## 3. Navigation: readiness, not a queue

The tree is a **prerequisite DAG**, not a line. `ac_concepto_prereq` holds hard edges (`strength ≥ 0.8`, a real gate) and soft edges (a suggestion, shown as a dotted line in the UI).

A gajo is in one of five states, and the whole `/aprender` screen is just these five states rendered:

| State | Meaning | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| `latente` | Prereqs unmet | Hairline outline, no fill, no interaction. Tooltip names the missing gajo. |
| `disponible` | Ready to start | Domain colour outline, pulsing tip |
| `en_curso` | Started, not mastered | Partial fill = mean mastery of its conceptos |
| `frondoso` | Mastered (mean mastery ≥ 0.85) | Full domain-colour fill, leaves rendered |
| `marchito` | Was mastered, decayed below 0.6 | Desaturated fill + a "regar" affordance |

**`marchito` is the retention engine and it is on-brand.** Brote already has `care_world()` and the "regá tu mundo" gesture. Knowledge that is not revisited wilts, exactly like the world dims when the streak breaks. Watering it = a review session. This makes spaced repetition a *thing you can see on a tree* rather than a hidden scheduler, which is a genuinely better UX than a "practice" button and is nobody else's design.

The user is never hard-blocked from a *rama* — only individual gajos gate on prerequisites, and the trunk gates everything at ring 1. There is always at least one `disponible` gajo and always at least one `marchito` one after week two, so the screen never dead-ends.

## 4. What a lesson actually is

**A hoja is a request, not a document.** When a user opens a hoja, the server composes a session:

```
academia_start_session(p_hoja_id)
  → picks 7–12 steps from the item pool
  → creates one `ac_entrega` (delivery) row per step, single-use, expiring
  → returns payloads WITHOUT answers
```

Composition rule (the "custom session" the brief asks for):

```
target session = 9 steps

  1 × microlectura        opener, only if the session introduces a new concepto
  ~45% conceptos nuevos   items for conceptos in this hoja not yet seen
  ~30% repaso vencido     items for conceptos with retrievability R < 0.9, most-forgotten first
  ~25% puntos débiles     items for conceptos with mastery between 0.3 and 0.7

  + every wrong answer is re-queued once at the end of the session
```

Ordering constraints, all cheap and all mandatory:

- Never two consecutive items from the same `plantilla` (template).
- Never more than two consecutive from the same `concepto`.
- Open with an item ~10 points easier than target; close on an item the user is likely to get right.
- Interleave ramas when the session is a `riego` (review) session — interleaving beats blocking on delayed tests, and a review session is precisely a delayed test.

**Difficulty targeting.** Per `(user, rama)` we keep an Elo-style ability `theta`; every item carries a difficulty `b`. Target first-try success **P\* = 0.82** (research puts the optimum at 0.80–0.85). Candidates are ranked by `−|b − b*|`, the top 8 are taken, and one is chosen at random among them (randomesque exposure control) so the pool does not collapse onto the same items.

**Personalisation inputs already present in `profiles`** — use all of them:

| Field | Effect |
|---|---|
| `interests[]` | Weights which rama is suggested next |
| `context` (`balcon`, `jardin`, `auto`, `bici`, `mascota`, `compra`) | Chooses the *variant slot* in an item template: a user with `mascota` gets the responsible-pet-ownership phrasing of the same concepto |
| `account_type` (`kid`/`teen`/`adult`) | Hard filter on `age_groups`, and selects the register of the copy variant |
| `city` / `neighborhood` | Chooses the local example (Riachuelo vs Delta vs costa) |
| `user_domain_points` | A user who acts in `residuos` gets `residuos` framing for cross-cutting conceptos |
| Past wrong answers | Feeds the weak-points slice and the misconception targeting |

Two users therefore never see the same session, and rarely the same sentence — not because a model writes it live, but because every item is a **template with slots** filled from a curated pool. That is the "nothing hardcoded" requirement, met cheaply, safely, and without a per-request LLM call.

## 5. Mastery and decay

One row per `(user, concepto)`. Two numbers, both cheap:

```
mastery_ema   ∈ [0,1]   exponential moving average of correctness
half_life     days      how long until recall drops to 50%

on answer (correct ∈ {0,1}):
    mastery_ema ← mastery_ema + α·(correct − mastery_ema)          α = 0.30
    half_life   ← clamp(half_life × (correct ? 2.2 : 0.45), 0.25, 365)

on read:
    R (retrievability) = 2 ^ ( −days_since_seen / half_life )
    fuerza (displayed strength) = mastery_ema × R
```

This is a deliberately simplified half-life model — the same shape as Duolingo's published half-life regression, without needing training data. `13-data-model.md` specifies the columns; Phase 3 may refine the constants from logged data, but **must not** replace the interface.

Mastery **decays and can regress**. A gajo that was `frondoso` becomes `marchito`. This is the correct model (Khan Academy's mastery levels regress too) and it is what keeps a finite tree generating infinite sessions.

Every answer is logged with enough context to recalibrate later — `(user, item, plantilla, concepto, delivery, chosen, correct, latency_ms, theta_before, b_before, issued_at, answered_at)`. Nothing above can be improved without that log, so it is written from day one.

## 6. The thing no competitor has: the action hook

Every hoja ends on a card that is not a score.

> **Ahora hacelo.**
> Aprendiste que una canilla que gotea puede perder 30 litros por día.
> → *Revisá las canillas de tu casa* · +50 pts · dominio Agua

It links to a real row in the existing `activities` catalogue, matched on `domain_slug` and the conceptos taught. Tapping it opens `/acciones/[slug]`, and completing it runs the existing `complete_activity` RPC — real XP, real impact numbers in `impact_water_l` / `impact_co2_kg`, real world growth.

This closes the loop the whole app is built on: **entender → hacer → ver crecer**. It is the reason the Academia belongs inside Brote rather than being a separate app, it is a structural feature no language app can copy, and it is the strongest originality argument in the whole design.

Rules: the suggested action must be `age_groups`-compatible, `min_rank_slug`-reachable, and not already completed inside its `repeat_cooldown_hours`. If nothing qualifies, show a rama-level suggestion instead of a broken link. Never fabricate an action.

## 7. Identity — how this looks like Brote and nothing else

Governed by `BROTE_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` ("Bitácora Viva": *a naturalist's field log with the quiet confidence of a financial terminal*). Applied to the Academia in `15-ui-motion.md`. The three identity anchors:

1. **The tree is a real drawing, not a list of circles.** Hand-tuned SVG, branches that actually fork, leaves that fill in. Vertical scroll, zoomable. Not a column of nodes with a dotted line.
2. **The field-log voice.** Every concepto card shows its source, like a field note shows its reference. Numbers use `<CountUp>` and the tabular `tnum` treatment. The dark "en vivo" strip from `PulseStrip` appears once per screen with a real stat (`conceptos frondosos`, `racha de riego`).
3. **Pip narrates, never lectures.** Pip appears at the session opener, at a wrong-answer recovery, and at the results — three beats, no more. Warm, brief, rioplatense, never guilt-tripping. Hope-and-agency framing is a hard content rule, not a tone preference: environmental-education research is clear that doom framing suppresses action, especially in kids.

Explicitly **not**: hearts, lives, an owl, a mascot that scolds you, a green that isn't Brote's green, a "streak freeze" by that name, league tiers copied from anywhere, or any node-path artwork resembling another app. See `AGENT-RULES.md` §5 and `research/01-duolingo-mechanics-and-ip.md`.

## 8. How it stays infinite

Three independent mechanisms, in increasing order of ambition. Phase 1 ships (1), Phase 3 ships (2) and (3).

1. **Combinatorial item yield.** A `plantilla` (item template) has slots — number, species, region, context, distractor family. A single template across 5 independent slot axes with 4 values each yields 1,024 distinct items from one authored object. 12 exercise types × ~450 conceptos × slot combinatorics = a pool that cannot be exhausted by a human, generated deterministically from a seed, stored as `(plantilla_id, seed)` rather than as rows.
2. **Pipeline top-up.** A nightly job finds `(concepto, tipo, difficulty band)` pools below a floor of 40 active items and asks Gemini — in batch, grounded in `ac_fuentes`, schema-validated, judged, human-reviewed — to fill them. See `14-generation-pipeline.md`.
3. **Anillo expansion.** When a user masters the reachable tree at ring *n*, the pipeline proposes new gajos and conceptos for ring *n+1* from the taxonomy: prompted with the parent rama, existing sibling gajos, and the anillo rubric; output lands as `status = 'propuesto'` and needs approval in `/panel`. The curriculum grows without anyone sitting down to author it, and nothing user-visible is ever unreviewed.

## 9. Success criteria

The section works if, six weeks after launch:

- A user who opens the Academia daily has never seen the same session twice.
- The median first-try correctness sits between **0.78 and 0.86** (if it drifts above, targeting is broken; below, the pool is mis-calibrated).
- At least 25% of Academia sessions end with the action hook being tapped.
- `marchito` gajos are being watered — i.e. review sessions are a real share of volume, not a dead feature.
- Zero user-reported factual errors that trace to a generated item without a source.
